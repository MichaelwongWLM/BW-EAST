Frequently asked questions

Can you provide specifications and certificates?

Available technical and facility documents are confirmed for the specific product and producing site during qualification.

Can you arrange samples?

Sample availability, quantity, preparation time and courier arrangements depend on the product and destination and are confirmed before dispatch.

What information is needed for a quotation?

For dehydrated ingredients, please send the specification, quantity, packing, destination and timing. For fresh produce, also include the product or variety, preferred origin or grade, seasonal window and cold-chain requirements.

Do the website photos define the product specification?

No. The photos are representative references. The written specification and commercial confirmation govern an order.
