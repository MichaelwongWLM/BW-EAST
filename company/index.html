Company

An ingredient supply partner based in Shandong.

Qingdao Best-Integrity Trade Co., Ltd. supports food and pet food ingredient buyers across a core dehydrated range, selected fresh produce and other raw materials.

Established in 2014

Clear work, careful follow-through.

Our work began with a straightforward idea: buyers should be able to understand what is being offered, which points still need confirmation and how an order will be followed.

We supply ingredients for food and pet food applications. Our established core range covers dehydrated vegetables and sweet potato ingredients, with formats assessed against the buyer's application and specification.

Being based in Shandong places us close to one of China's established fruit and vegetable production regions. Selected fresh fruit and vegetable enquiries, together with other raw material requirements, are reviewed case by case according to product, season or specification, packing, destination and delivery requirements.

Rather than presenting every item as identical, we review the product and producing facility relevant to each inquiry. This helps keep samples, technical documents and commercial commitments connected to the actual supply route.
